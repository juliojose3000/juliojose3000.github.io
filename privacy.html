Privacy Policy: Claude Gmail

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Claude Gmail is a personal tool. Its only user is its owner, Julio Segura.

What data it accesses

With the owner's consent, it reads email from the owner's Gmail accounts and files from his Google Drive accounts through Google's APIs.

How the data is used

The data is used only to help the owner manage his own email and personal finances. It is processed on the owner's own computer.

Sharing

The data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and deletion

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's computer. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
